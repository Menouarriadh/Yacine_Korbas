About Me

The mind behind the medicine

I'm Yacine Korbas, a 25 year old medical content creator passionate about making science accessible. What started as curiosity turned into a mission: bridge the gap between complex medical knowledge and everyday understanding.

My content covers everything from laser eye surgery to cellular biology always backed by research, always explained simply. I believe that when people understand their bodies, they make better health decisions.
